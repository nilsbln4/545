########## Problem 4 ##########

Sample covariance matrix (block 1 = x1, block 2 = x2)
=====================================================
S11 = Var(x1)  = 1.099875
S22 = Var(x2)  = 4.104749
S12 = S21 = Cov(x1,x2) = 1.696902

(a) Conditional variance of x2 given x1
=======================================
Formula: Var(x2|x1) = S22 - S21 * S11^-1 * S12
Var(x2|x1)                = 1.486746
Var(x2) (unconditional)   = 4.104749
factor = Var(x2|x1)/Var(x2) = 1 - rho^2 = 0.362201  (rho = 0.798623)

(b) Does the factor depend on the observed x1?
==============================================
No: S21 * S11^-1 * S12 has no x1 term in it, so the conditional
variance -- and hence the factor above -- is the same for every x1.

(c) Conditional mean of x2 given x1
===================================
Formula: E(x2|x1) = mu2 + S21 * S11^-1 * (x1 - mu1)
slope on x1 (= S21/S11, the OLS slope of x2~x1) = 1.542813
intercept implied                                = 0.071073
